# Savoy Ventures market comparison

Research checked September 10, 2026. These are small public samples, not representative market rates or proof that a new seller will win business. Posted budgets are asking budgets, not verified earnings. Search queries included explicit budget amounts during discovery, so results cannot support claims about typical prices.

## Decision

Keep catalog cleanup as a prepared experiment, not a final business selection. The evidence does not justify describing it as the most profitable gap. Compare it against a narrowly scoped recurring reporting service after learning the owner's skills, contacts, hours, and appetite for technical support. Defer building a public storefront until the owner can identify the business and the service they can deliver.

## Alternative worth testing

Proposed Savoy Reporting Desk: convert an agency's approved monthly exports into a repeatable client report with reconciled totals, charts, and a list of questions for the account manager. Begin with a single report and a fixed input format. Keep analysis tied to supplied facts and require the account manager to approve recommendations before client delivery.

A paid pilot should measure input variability, revision time, and how many hours the agency actually saves. Only then quote recurring work. Recurring revenue is attractive for a weekly income target, but monthly retainers do not mean weekly cash receipts. Do not offer unlimited reports or imply unattended automation until the workflow has been tested against missing and malformed inputs.

The automation marketplace evidence supports investigating this problem, not a claim that the reporting service above has buyers at any proposed retainer. A separate listing promises possible $500–1,000 future projects after a $15 demonstration; those future amounts are explicitly conditional and should not be counted as available contracted revenue.
