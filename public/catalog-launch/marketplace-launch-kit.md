# Savoy Ventures Marketplace Launch Kit

## Operating rule

Use Fiverr for fixed-scope inbound packages and Upwork only for selective jobs that clearly match a packaged service. Do not scrape either marketplace, submit bulk proposals, promise marketplace approval, or move pre-contract conversations or payment off-platform.

## Fiverr launch order

### Amazon Listing File Rescue — $299

**Gig title:** I will repair your Amazon listing file and product variations

Repair up to 25 listing or variation rows. Includes an upload-ready file, parent-child relationship review, required-attribute review, exception report, change log, and one revision. Marketplace approval, ranking, and sales performance are not guaranteed.

### Menu-to-Online Catalog Conversion — $199

**Gig title:** I will convert your restaurant menu into structured online catalog data

Convert one customer-owned PDF, image set, or spreadsheet of up to 100 menu items. Includes structured categories, names, descriptions, prices, option groups, image mapping, missing-information report, and one revision. The customer confirms prices, allergens, taxes, and publishing requirements.

### Product Image Production Pack — $149

**Gig title:** I will prepare 25 consistent ecommerce product images

Prepare up to 25 customer-owned images through background cleanup, cropping, resizing, compression, SKU-safe naming, and an image manifest. Includes one revision. Complex retouching, new photography, and images without usage rights are excluded.

### Supplier File Normalization — $250

**Gig title:** I will clean and normalize your supplier product spreadsheet

Normalize up to three files or 2,500 rows into a canonical master file with source mapping, duplicate report, rejected-row report, validation summary, reusable import template, and one revision.

## Selective Upwork workflow

1. Review only jobs matching one of the six published service scopes.
2. Reject jobs requiring account sharing before a contract, unverifiable claims, prohibited automation, regulated professional advice, or guaranteed marketplace outcomes.
3. Score scope clarity, buyer history, budget fit, delivery feasibility, reusable value, and payment protection from 0–100.
4. Draft a proposal only at a score of 70 or higher.
5. Keep communication and payment on Upwork until its rules permit otherwise.
6. Open an SVOS delivery job after a contract and customer files are available.

## Proposal template

I reviewed the scope and the specific issue appears to be **[observed problem]**. Savoy Ventures can deliver **[fixed output]** for **[price/milestone]** within **[turnaround]**, assuming the supplied files remain within **[limit]**. You will receive the corrected output, an exception report, and a change log so the work is reviewable. Before starting, I would confirm **[one material question]**.

## Qualification gates

- The buyer has a visible and specific operational problem.
- The requested output matches an existing Savoy Ventures service.
- Source files and rights are available.
- The work can be completed within the stated boundary.
- Expected gross margin is at least 70% before founder time.
- No fabricated claims, prohibited automation, or guaranteed third-party outcomes are required.

## Revenue rule

An inquiry, proposal, checkout visit, or unsigned agreement is pipeline. Revenue is recorded only after Stripe or the marketplace confirms payment.
